Executive summary

NectarGuard Biotech is building biological soil-regeneration products for terrace gardens and farms across Maharashtra and India: NutriPrime, the Plant Booster series (GrowMax, FreshMax, BloomCare), StressGuard (Alpha, DroughtMagic), and a crop protection range coming soon. It sells to terrace growers, horticulture farmers and dealers, backs products with lab-tested, declared composition, and offers agronomist and soil-testing support. As it grows, it needs systems that match how it works. We propose a connected platform in three levels, each one growing on the last.

🌰Level 1

Core Operations System

Stock, purchases, sales, billing and payments in one place.

🌱Level 2

Traceability, Dealer & Grower Management

Batch and lab proof, dealer network, grower and trial records.

🌻Level 3

Digital Growth Platform with Automation & AI

Online store, portals, automated follow-ups and AI insights.

This overview is based on your public website. We will refine it together after understanding your actual workflows.

Our understanding of your business

What we gathered from your public information.

🧴Products

A growing family of soil and plant inputs in different pack sizes (for example, 30 kg NutriPrime bags), with variants matched to crop stages.

👩‍🌾Customers

Terrace growers (kits), horticulture farmers (farm packs, 1-acre trials) and dealers.

✅Quality promise

Composition is tested and declared bag by bag, and the biology is scientifically identified. Batch records and lab reports are central to the brand.

💬Sales channels

Direct sales, online enquiries (WhatsApp-first), consultations and a dealer network.

🚜Growth path

New products (crop protection), more crops and regions, more growers, and a production campus that is scaling up.

Start with Option 2: Traceability, Dealer & Grower Management

Important scope notes

This overview is based on public information and will be refined after a discovery discussion. The final scope may change after confirming:

  • Number of users, plants or production units, and warehouses

  • Lab test parameters to be linked to each batch

  • GST and invoice requirements
  • Online sales and shipping requirements

  • Accounting software integration
  • WhatsApp, SMS and email integrations
  • Existing data migration
  • Mobile app requirements and languages (English, Hindi, Marathi)
  • Hosting and deployment requirements
  • Additional custom reports

Third-party subscriptions, API usage, messaging services, payment gateways, hosting, domains and hardware are handled separately unless explicitly included in the final Scope of Work.
